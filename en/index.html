About

Biography

Dr. Han-Chou Lin is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Information Management at Ming Chuan University. He previously served as Project Assistant Professor in the Bachelor Program of Big Data Management and Application at National Ilan University, where he was also Deputy Executive Director of Institutional Research (IR), and as Project Assistant Professor in the Department of Business Administration at National Taipei University of Business. He has also held adjunct posts at National Chengchi University and Fo Guang University.

His expertise lies in artificial intelligence, data mining and financial time-series forecasting. His doctoral work proposed a fusion-and-optimization framework for hybrid forecasting models combining EMD, PSR, LSSVR, ELM and PSO, published in SSCI/SCI journals including Computational Economics and the International Journal on Artificial Intelligence Tools. His recent work extends to ESG and corporate financial performance, text mining of customer reviews, deep-learning ensembles and explainable AI (SHAP, LIME).

In the classroom he emphasizes hands-on, cross-disciplinary learning through project-based learning, service learning and industry partnerships. His students have built database and visualization systems for local businesses and won several project and service-learning awards. He also serves as Vice President of the Yilan County SME Honorary Advisors Association, helping local firms with digital transformation.

Research

Interests

My research applies AI and data mining to financial forecasting, management analytics and sustainable development. It pairs theoretical models with real-world data and uses hybrid modelling strategies to improve forecasting accuracy. I am currently designing and comparing explainable-AI models, with the aim of building transparent and socially impactful AI decision frameworks for FinTech, sustainable business and smart management.

01

Hybrid Models for Financial Time Series

Combining signal-decomposition techniques (EMD, HHT, phase-space reconstruction, chaos theory) with SVR, LSSVR, ELM, random forests and LSTM, and using PSO for parameter optimization and weighted fusion, to make exchange-rate and stock-index forecasts more robust and accurate.

02

ESG & Sustainable Finance

Using XGBoost, MLP and LSTM models to analyze the lagged effects of ESG dimensions on corporate revenue and stock prices, and developing sustainable-investment recommendation models that integrate ESG indicators with green-finance concepts.

03

Text Mining & Smart Consumer Behavior

Applying LDA topic models, TF-IDF and sentiment analysis to online reviews to surface latent customer needs, and studying how AI-extracted nutrition information shapes consumers' health choices and purchasing behavior.

04

Explainable AI & Customer Value Prediction

Building hybrid deep-learning ensembles (DNN + LSTM) for multi-output customer-value prediction, with SHAP and LIME for interpretability and transparent managerial decisions, and using GANs to simulate stock-price dynamics.
